♡ Backups & privacy

Important: Data is saved in this browser's local storage on this device. It is not encrypted. Anyone with access to your browser profile/device may be able to read it. Private browsing, clearing site data, changing browsers, or losing the device can erase entries. Make regular backups and store them somewhere private.
